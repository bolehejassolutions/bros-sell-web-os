# BROS SELL™ — Content Generation & Replenishment Standard

**Version:** 2026-10-11 · **Status:** CURRENT / supersedes earlier creative prompts for NEW assets only  
**Scope:** Organic content, future editorial bank entries and visual QA for Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.  
**Authority:** Latest owner-approved Project Sources and actual release assets outrank this document. This standard outranks older launch calendars, discontinued Metricool instructions and prior generic poster prompts. Existing scheduled/sent Buffer posts and the approved Millennial vs Gen Z A/B experiment are **not** retroactively edited.

## 1. Non-negotiable brand and truth

- Product: **BROS SELL™ — Closing OS**. Doctrine: **“Menjelaskan, bukan Memujuk.”**
- Audience: Malaysian sellers, entrepreneurs, business owners and sales operators. **Do not narrow** brand positioning to micro-businesses only.
- Current approved owner-facing price: **RM100 until further notice**. Avoid making checkout-total or promotion/discount claims unless separately verified. Do not revive RM99, RM399, September deadlines, “first 100”, or a fake limited offer.
- Natural, contemporary **Bahasa Melayu Malaysia**. Keep English only when it helps comprehension. Sound like actual seller/customer conversations, not policy manuals, classroom prose, robotic scripts or generic motivation.
- Use **only the exact approved BROS SELL™ logo files**, unchanged in mark, words, colours, proportions and ™. If the correct asset is not technically available, hold branded visual publication rather than invent a logo.
- No fabricated customer examples disguised as actual events, testimonials, proof, screenshots, conversion numbers, buyer quotes, revenue, product functions, guarantees or urgency. Label fictional dialogues as illustrative. Respect privacy, consent, buyer autonomy and Islamic values.
- One compelling selling problem and one genuinely useful takeaway per asset. Explain the customer's decision, not pressure the customer. Avoid quotation-only editorial bias.

## 2. Creative direction — separate production sessions

**Target:** Each consecutive visual should plausibly look like a **different real-world production session**, not a recycled black-card template or the same layout with replaced headlines.

For each NEW asset, record an **archetype**: documentary selling moment, natural mobile UGC, working desk/hand notes, seller/customer dialogue reconstruction, whiteboard teaching, photojournalistic retail/service scene, print/editorial explanation, annotated realistic photo, factual workflow demonstration, product explanation or another relevant archetype.

Review the **previous five released assets**. A candidate should change **at least six of these ten dimensions** relative to its nearest recent creative: (1) subject; (2) camera/framing; (3) setting; (4) lighting; (5) format; (6) text density; (7) typography hierarchy; (8) texture/material treatment; (9) narrative device; (10) content mechanism. A different caption, headline, colour, pose or crop by itself does **not** pass. Diversity must be visible, not merely claimed in metadata.

Prefer recognisable Malaysian situations (shop counters, service discussions, a credible desk, WhatsApp-style selling when recreated and clearly illustrative), natural light, believable props and restrained production. Do not fake screenshots, manufacture customer data, or present AI-imagined environments as photographic evidence. A flat typographic educational card is allowed **when it is the right format**; it must not be every asset.

**Important current-system limitation:** The live `render.py` produces identical-structure typography cards and `content.mjs` contains **60 finite human-written seeds**. This worker does **not** run generative image or language models, and cannot currently meet the six-of-ten visual diversity gate for unattended scheduled production. Do **not** describe it as a continuous AI creative generator, and do not quietly switch it to paid AI generation. Existing approved queue remains untouched; the standard is a governance target for a separately tested replacement/generation pipeline.

## 3. Editorial architecture

**Selling moment → curiosity → reframe → diagnosis → system thinking → BROS SELL principle → useful action → optional product CTA.** Not every short post needs every stage, but the explanation must earn any product mention.

Rotate across target, buyer, offer, lead, qualification, value, closing, follow-up, post-sale and operations; balance new sellers who need practical foundations against experienced sellers improving their closing process. Focus on familiar real-life situations, including price questions, silence after a reply, “mahal”, “nak fikir dulu”, missing context, permission-based follow-ups and clear next steps. Treat examples as examples. Keep calls to action relevant, often educational; never hard-sell every post.

## 4. Platform adaptations — not resized duplicates

| Channel | Native treatment | Primary intent |
|---|---|---|
| Facebook | Contextual human-sounding paragraph or short seller conversation; image only where it adds clarity | Replies, informed discussion and genuine enquiries |
| Instagram | Strong image-first opening or readable carousel, uncluttered frames, caption expands the value | Saves, shares, qualified interest |
| TikTok | Immediate situation-led hook, 9:16 photo carousel/storyboard when video is unavailable; short natural copy | Attention and meaningful completion/response |

Check actual platform media requirements before submission. Do not claim slideshow images are videos. Include accessible alt text where supported. Adapt opening, pacing and information density, not just dimensions.

## 5. Millennial vs Gen Z language tests

Run parallel **voice** experiments on the same factual sales problem without stereotyping, and keep the product/claims/offer identical. Millennial variant may use a grounded, fuller conversational explanation; Gen Z variant may use shorter, direct phrases and immediate relatable context. Both must sound like Malaysians in ordinary conversation, not caricatures. Vary **one meaningful factor** per matched platform/topic pair where possible; store voice label, hypothesis, scheduled slot, asset ID and measured outcomes.

The approved 13–24 October 2026 A/B post calendar and its Google Sheet remain separate operational sources. Do not duplicate, reschedule, replace, or combine those entries with regular content bank tasks. **Copy style alone does not prove the audience's age**; demographic inference requires actual platform evidence. Declare a winner only after comparable, mature metrics, and never invent engagement or sales.

## 6. Replenishment specification (no fake infinite generation)

1. **Read:** Current approved BROS product/communication sources, existing published and scheduled Buffer posts, state branch package IDs/content hashes, latest QA decisions, and A/B exclusions.
2. **Propose:** A batch of *original* scenario-led candidates, each with a unique ID, topic, sales stage, audience state, locally natural hook, reframe, diagnostic question, practical next move, brand-safe CTA, visual archetype, 10-dimension vector, platform copies and factual source/provenance.
3. **Deduplicate:** Compare normalised headline, scenario, insight, lesson, visual layout and existing package IDs against **all** published/queued/retired content. New wording for the same idea is insufficient.
4. **Check content:** Source truth, practical usefulness, Malaysian register, Islamic/brand safety, synthetic-scenario labelling, pricing, claims, character limits, assets and genuine platform fitness.
5. **Check visual:** Verify that the actual rendered/exported media (not a description) differs in 6/10 dimensions and passes visual readability, crop/safe-area, unchanged logo checksum, plausible context, no deceptive UI and no overlap.
6. **Stage:** Only accepted original candidates enter a versioned bank with immutable media hashes. Preserve provenance and avoid changing already scheduled/sent Buffer posts. Separate generation from scheduling.
7. **Release:** Run dry-run tests, protect exact publication slots (05:00, 11:00, 17:00, 23:00 MYT), keep Buffer quota reserve and reconcile uncertain requests. Require explicit approval for new paid services, commercial changes or a material switch to unsupervised generative publishing.
8. **Learn:** Review actual status, URL and delivery timestamp before claiming sent; measure helpful replies/enquiries where evidence exists. Use conclusions from A/B only after its observation window. Feed lessons into the next *original* batch.

### Minimum candidate record

`contentId | topic | stage | audienceState | voiceVariant | sellingMoment | hook | reframe | diagnosticQuestion | actionableLesson | archetype | visualVector10 | platformCopies | brandAssetHash | visualAssetHashes | claimsSources | scenarioDisclosure | dedupeFingerprint | qaStatus | dueAt | BufferPostIds | deliveryEvidence`

**Hard fail:** copied scenario, unsupported claim, obsolete price, incorrect logo, false customer evidence, unclear next move, unreal visual metadata, media unreadable, six-of-ten requirement unverified, or collision with an A/B slot. Hold the candidate; do not auto-publish it.
